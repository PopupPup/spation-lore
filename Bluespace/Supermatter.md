At extreme pressures and temperatures all matter becomes what is known as supermatter. Supermatter is extremely unstable by default. Supermatter is defined not by its realspace properties, but its bluespace properties. Supermatter warps bluespace in a controlled way. It pulls bluespace metaphorically closer to realspace meaning bluespace intersecting with realspace becomes far more common. If supermatter is present for an extended period of time, such as inside of some stars, it can stabilize, manifesting as apparent crystallization in realspace. This occurs when the tide of bluespace causes an extremely prolonged intersection between realspace and bluespace. Stabilized bluespace pulls bluespace and realspace so close together that any sophontic activity causes intersection. As such, it effectively creates a hole between realspace and bluespace through which things can slip through. Stabilized bluespace, when left alone, also creates bluespace activity of its own. With enough time, this activity will develop into a psyche in spread across bluespace and realspace and reinforced itself through the interaction between the two.
