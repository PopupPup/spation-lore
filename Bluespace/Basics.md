The following is **not** known fully by any characters.

Bluespace is a field that interacts with consciousness. Interactions with bluespace by sophonts are not returned equally- there is a gap between realspace and bluespace that bluespace usually cannot cross, even though it is influenced by sophonts. Bluespace can be imagined like a 2d plane, similar to how gravity is imagined. This plane is laid over realspace, also imagined as a 2d plane. Usually the gap between realspace and bluespace is large enough that bluespace cannot intersect with realspace. Sophonts, however, impact bluespace and a sufficient density of consciousness can cause the impacts on bluespace to constructively interfere enough to cause an intersection between bluespace and realspace. As the psyches of sophonts tend not to be linked so this interference tends to alternate between constructive and destructive causing the intersection between bluespace and realspace to be extremely short-lived and sporadic. These intersections create anomalies. Anomalies are physically impossible to create within realspace alone. They can break the laws of physics and are extremely powerful as a result.

Bluespace is not static when left alone. There is a "tide" in bluespace of unimaginable scale. As such, there are certain areas of realspace that are "closer" to bluespace. While these areas move they do so on such unimaginably large timescales that they are effectively static.
