Artifacts are the remnants of the knowledge of a dead civilization that mastered bluespace completely. This civilization completely incorporated itself into bluespace and ceased to exist in realspace, creating the bluespace tide. In this way all bluespace is [sophontic bluespace](obsidian://open?vault=Obsidian%20Vault&file=spation-lore%2FSpecies%2FSophontic%20Bluespace). Artifacts are created when constructive interference with the psyches of nearby sophonts and the bluespace tide amplifies a certain piece of this old civilization's knowledge. This creates a realspace manifestation of said knowledge in the form of a strange item that can impact reality by creating well-known elements, flora, fauna, or reactions like explosions. Rarely this artifacts will also create the perception of unintelligible whispers in an unknown language in nearby sophonts. These whispers contain knowledge not yet known by enough nearby sophonts to get amplified and manifest in realspace.
